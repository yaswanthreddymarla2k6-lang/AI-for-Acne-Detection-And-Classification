Detectable Acne Types

Our AI system can identify and classify five major types of acne lesions

Blackheads

Open comedones with dark surface due to oxidized melanin

Whiteheads

Closed comedones with white or flesh-colored surface

Papules

Small, solid, raised lesions without pus

Pustules

Inflamed lesions containing pus with white center

Cysts

Deep, painful, pus-filled lesions under the skin
