About Our Project

Welcome to the AI Acne Detection System! This is a simple project built to detect and classify different types of acne from uploaded images using basic Artificial Intelligence.

🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is to build an easy-to-use website where users can upload a picture of their skin and get a quick prediction of what kind of acne it might be, using a trained AI model.

🧠 How It Works

We use a machine learning model to look at images and recognize patterns. The model has been trained on pictures of common skin conditions like whiteheads and blackheads.

🔍 Quick Results

Just upload a photo, and the system will process it instantly. It will try to guess the type of acne and show you its prediction right on the screen.

🛡️ Safe & Simple

This is a simple student project. Whatever images you upload are only processed to show you the result and aren't saved anywhere permanently.

👨‍⚕️ Disclaimer

Remember, this is just a basic project! It is not a real doctor. If you have serious skin concerns, you should always consult a real dermatologist.
